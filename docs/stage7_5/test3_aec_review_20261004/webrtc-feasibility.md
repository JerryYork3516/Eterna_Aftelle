## Why a single source-backed repair cannot yet be specified

1. **The present AEC path has a measured negative failure.** In the optimized physical negative, old short-delay votes dominate the pre-echo histogram even when repeated later-lag matched-filter observations exist. At frame 688, the independent source-code reconstruction has 152 block-2 votes versus 36 block-26 votes. During false qualification at 650–652, AEC uses causal, continuous content about 24 ms behind capture and leaves much of the resident echo in clean output. The Host then takes three `adaptive_double_talk` frames and opens at 652. This is a real execution-chain failure, not a missing/zero/future reference in that interval.
2. **The apparent AEC repair breaks safety and coverage elsewhere.** Directly reporting current main lag makes the optimized physical negative 0/0 in isolated replay, but on the higher-volume software-near capsule it jumps 3→49→99 AEC blocks from one new reliable observation per new block, holds block 99 for about 1.2 seconds, forwards zero injected-near spans, and opens 38 pure-echo frames before injection. Its normal-volume apparent pass occurs while the AEC controller has no applied delay during the positive interval. This second field-choice attempt was preregistered and rejected.
3. **The older physical negative contains different failures.** Its four false Gate starts include a stale short lock at 589, no accepted delay at 298/689, and missing causal render plus repeated underrun/reset at 790. A rule that only replaces a stale short lag cannot cover all four without a separate qualification policy. That policy must preserve long and short human speech; the existing software injection cannot establish that.
4. **Existing proposed shortcuts already have concrete counterexamples.** A fixed 27-block oracle worsened the physical pre-event and later false windows; 32-block filter support worsened the optimized negative; external-delay mode and render-event retention failed their registered screens. Removing adaptive double-talk made the optimized negative 0/0 but cut software-near forwarding 341→171 and 352→63. Requiring a fresh matched-filter winner on every confirmation frame loses the existing long-software-positive second opening. A 250 ms continuous raw-correlation veto overlaps pre-injection echo with software-near ranges and loses the causal reference at old frame 790. The alternative-echo path fails at its unchanged clean-unexplained-energy condition when AEC itself leaves strong resident echo; raising that condition to fit the negative is prohibited.
5. **The remaining ERLE proposal is precondition-false.** Its veto requires a `>=3 dB` ERLE observation within the previous 30 frames. Independently refreshed AEC metrics have no such observation before optimized false frames 652/694/715 (all current ERLE about 0.1755 dB; prior maximum only 2.7415 dB before frame 735). The older physical negative never reaches that prerequisite. Higher-volume software-near remains about 0.1755 dB throughout its injection; normal software-near is below 1 dB for 306/325 injection frames. Thus this veto cannot cover the physical failure as specified, and its claimed positive exemption is unsupported. Do not implement it or remove its precondition to force a pass.

The above rejects the **specified** candidates and a new rule chosen from the observed numbers. It does not prove that a different AEC/source-attribution method cannot work. In particular, high raw/render correlation means an echo component is present; it does not prove the person is absent during double-talk. Low processed energy can mean successful echo removal **or** erased user speech. Neither signal alone grants source identity.
